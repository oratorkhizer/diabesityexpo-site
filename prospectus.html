The event

One day where Hyderabad gets its tests done, with the doctors in the room.

The Caspian Diabesity Expo is the city's public day of diabetes and obesity action. In 2026 the format changes: visitors book a Metabolic Check Pass, have the full Caspian Metabolic Profile (thyroid, CBC, ESR, liver, kidney, HbA1c, urine, vitamin D, B12, iron, lipids, body composition) drawn at the expo without fasting, and a doctor reviews the report with them. Around that core sit free basic screening for every visitor, specialist talks, hands-on workshops, a family programme and the solutions exhibition.

Who is in the hall

  • People living with diabetes or obesity, and the family members who cook for them
  • Adults at risk who have never been tested: the largest and most valuable group for prevention brands
  • Corporate HR teams buying check-ups for staff, and CSR desks funding free screenings

Why the audience is different

Every paid visitor has spent ₹1,999 or more on their own health that morning and is waiting for a report. Nobody is browsing. They ask questions, try products and sign up. Partners who sample, demonstrate and capture leads at the stall consistently outperform those who only display.

CSR and corporate wellness

CSR through the Foundation

Companies fund free metabolic checks for low-income visitors through the Caspian Healthcare Foundation under Schedule VII (preventive healthcare). Each full metabolic screening is valued at ₹1,000 to the beneficiary.

Every CSR partner receives a board-ready impact report within 45 days of the event (screenings done, abnormal results found and referred, anonymised cohort data) and audited utilisation. Receipts and the Foundation's registration details are shared on request.

Corporate wellness block

₹1,799 per employee, minimum 25. Your team gets the full Metabolic Check Pass (12 tests, no fasting, doctor review, lunch, reserved seating) on one invoice. HR receives an anonymised cohort report (prevalence of high HbA1c, blood pressure, lipids and low vitamin D across the group) and a talk slot for a wellness session on the day. Receipts qualify as preventive health check-ups under Section 80D for employees who pay themselves.

A note for pharmaceutical partners

The expo is a patient-facing event; no promotional activity is directed at patients, in line with UCPMP 2024. Pharmaceutical companies take part through CSR-funded free screenings via the Foundation and through their consumer health, nutrition and device divisions at the stalls and special zones.

What every partner receives

  • A written reach report within 45 days: footfall, screenings, stall visits, press and social coverage
  • Photography and video of your presence, cleared for your own use
  • Opt-in lead sharing for Title and Co-Powered partners, with visitor consent recorded at registration
  • Right of first refusal for the same package in 2027
