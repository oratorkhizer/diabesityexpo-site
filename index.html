One day, four points of view

Whoever you are in this story, you leave further ahead.

Diabesity touches the person living with it, the family around them, the friend postponing a test, and the institutions that can fund the fight. The expo is designed for all of them.

Self

You live with diabetes or obesity

You arriveManaging mostly alone between appointments, with questions that never fit into a ten minute consult.

You leave with

The year's metabolic check done, a doctor's reading of it, tools you tried yourself, and a clear next step for the year ahead.

Other

You care for someone who does

You arriveWorried, cooking every meal with doubt, doing your best without training.

You leave with

The skills of a caregiver: checking feet at home, spotting a sugar crash, fixing the kitchen. And your own screening done, because risk runs in families.

Observer

You keep postponing the test

You arriveHealthy, as far as you know. Thirty something, desk job, a parent with diabetes, never tested.

You leave with

Your numbers, known for the first time, what they actually mean, and exactly what to change so this never becomes your disease.

Institution

You lead a brand, CSR desk or institution

You arriveWith a mandate: reach, leads, or verified public health impact.

You leave with

Up to 2,000 health-intent visitors met face to face, qualified leads, an audited impact report, and your name on the city's flagship World Diabetes Day event. Partner with us.

The day itself

Everything that helps, under one roof.

Screening for everyone, the full check for those who book

Random sugar, blood pressure and BMI are free for every visitor. Book a Metabolic Check Pass and the full 12-test profile is drawn at the expo, with a doctor to read the report with you.

Straight answers from specialists

Talks in simple language on obesity, diabetic foot, vaccination and what is real about reversal. Ask anything, no consult fee.

Hands-on workshops

Foot care you can do at home, reading a CGM, plate planning for an Indian kitchen, and recognising emergencies early.

The solutions expo

CGMs and glucometers, healthy foods, diabetes apps, footwear, fitness and insurance. Try before you decide, with no pressure.

The Report Clinic

Already have this year's reports? Bring them. A doctor sits with you for ten minutes and tells you what the numbers mean and what to do next.

A family day out

Free entry for the whole household, lunch for every registered visitor, tea and water all day, and programming that welcomes parents, partners and kids.

Questions

Before you come

Is entry really free?

Yes. Register for a Free Floor Pass and walk in to the talks, workshops and stalls, with a free basic screening (random sugar, BP, BMI), lunch, and tea and water through the day. Paid passes add the full metabolic check-up, the doctor review, dinner and reserved seating. Nobody is turned away.

What does the Metabolic Check include, and do I need to fast?

The full Caspian Metabolic Profile: thyroid profile, CBC, ESR, liver and kidney function, HbA1c, urine examination, vitamin D, vitamin B12, iron profile, lipid profile and body composition analysis. Blood is drawn at the expo, no fasting needed. The report reaches you on WhatsApp the next working day, and a doctor reviews it with you by video or at Caspian within the week. Basic screening (random sugar, BP, BMI) stays free for every visitor.

Is the check-up eligible under Section 80D?

Yes. Preventive health check-up expenses qualify for deduction under Section 80D, up to ₹5,000 a year within the overall limit. Keep the receipt issued for your pass.

Can I bring my parents and children?

Please do. The expo is built as a family day. The Couple and Family Passes give everyone their own Metabolic Check and one joint consult, because risk runs in families.

Are paid passes refundable?

Yes, fully refundable until 31 October 2026. Refunds are processed to the original payment method through Razorpay. See the refund policy in the footer.
